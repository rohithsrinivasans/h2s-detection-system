H2S Exposure Detection

Capture or upload a photo of the worker's lead acetate indicator paper. The CNN classifies its color-change stage and logs an hourly exposure reading.

On a phone this opens the camera directly. On desktop, browse for a file.

Color-stage legend

  • No exposure — off-white / cream
  • Low — faint tan / yellow tinge
  • Medium — visible brown
  • High — dark brown / black

ppm ranges shown are placeholders — calibrate against certified H2S gas standards for your specific paper batch before relying on them for safety decisions.
